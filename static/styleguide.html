Probability Bureau — living style guide

css/polyclawd.css v1 · adopt with 2 lines (see CLAUDE.md → Design System)

A mid-century forecast bureau rebuilt as a terminal. Warm carbon chart paper, paper ink, square corners, hairline borders, one orange. Everything on this page is rendered by the system itself — if it looks wrong here, fix the CSS, not the page. Banned: Inter, border-radius, glow shadows, glassmorphism, decorative gradients, new font pairings.
