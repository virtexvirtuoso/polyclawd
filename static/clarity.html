Methodology: Passage probability is term-matched — we pick the single Kalshi ladder rung whose close date is closest to the longest-dated live Polymarket passage market, then take a volume-weighted average across that matched set. Naive averaging across a 9-rung ladder would collapse different horizons into an uninterpretable number. Expired markets are excluded.

Gate-decomposition model: CLARITY passage is modeled as a sequence of AND-gated procedural gates — Senate Agriculture committee markup and Senate Banking committee markup (both jurisdictions required for a split SEC/CFTC bill), then Senate floor vote (60-vote cloture + majority passage), then House concurrence on amended text (or conference committee if Senate passes its own vehicle like S. 4064 / DCIA), then presidential signature. The Kalshi ladder above prices the joint probability of clearing every remaining gate before each rung's deadline. See H.R. 3633 on congress.gov and the plain-English explainer for the full framework.
